# Project overview

**Project:** DIY Submarine · **Group:** 5 · **Where:** Makerspace, Sanjivani University

**Team:** Autade Yashraj, Viraj Patare, Pranav Kathale, Rushikesh Chinchane  
**Project guide:** Dr. Kiran Wakchaure

## Problem

Looking under water — tanks, wells, ponds, canals, hulls — is risky for people and expensive with commercial ROVs, which are also closed systems that are hard to learn from.

## Aim

Build a small, tethered underwater vehicle from off-the-shelf uPVC pipe fittings and small DC motors, end to end: CAD model, sealed hull, propulsion, surface wiring and water testing.

## Question

Can a student team build a watertight, controllable underwater vehicle from plumbing parts — and what ballast, sealing and thrust does it need to go from floating to controlled diving?

## Scope of the current prototype

- Sealed uPVC hull, fixed end cap, threaded access cap
- Sealed DC-motor thruster pods with propellers, strapped to the hull
- Tethered power and switching from the surface
- Tank test 01: floating check

Not yet in scope: onboard camera, depth sensing, autonomous control.
