# Testing and results

## Tank test 01 — float check

Video: [`media/video/Tank_Test.mp4`](../media/video/Tank_Test.mp4)

Observed in the footage:
- The hull, launched by hand, floats horizontally at the surface.
- The top of the hull stays above the waterline — strongly positive buoyancy.
- The thruster pod rides under the hull, held by the straps; the tether runs to the tank edge.

Not yet shown: submerged running, depth control, steering, leak check after a dive.

**No measured results yet.**
