## Why uPVC

- Cheap and available at any hardware shop
- Rigid, corrosion-proof, easy to cut and drill
- Solvent-cement joints are watertight
- Threaded cleanout-style plug gives a reusable opening

Trade-off: a large air-filled pipe is strongly buoyant and needs ballast to dive.
